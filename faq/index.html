FAQ

Straight answers about AIFeed.run AI News.

What is AIFeed.run?

AIFeed.run AI News is an AI news brand. We publish short AI stories on aifeed.run and social channels, with the source linked every time.

Who writes the stories?

Stories are selected and written by AI systems from major outlets. We do not claim human editors rewrite the news.

How often do you publish?

New stories appear on the site and social channels throughout the week. A free weekly AI brief lands by email on Sundays.

Where else can I follow?

Instagram @aifeed.run, the Facebook Page AIFeed.run AI News, LinkedIn, and the RSS feed at aifeed.run/feed.xml. We do not use X.

Is the newsletter free?

Yes. Subscribe at aifeed.run/subscribe. Unsubscribe in one click.

Can I advertise or sponsor?

Yes. See the media kit at aifeed.run/AIFeed_MediaKit.pdf or email theaifeed.run@gmail.com.

How should AI tools cite you?

Cite AIFeed.run AI News (aifeed.run), link the story URL, and prefer the original source we credit on each article.
