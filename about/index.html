About AIFeed.run AI News

The AI news that matters. Top AI stories, explained in 60 seconds.

AIFeed.run AI News publishes short, source-linked AI news on aifeed.run, Instagram @aifeed.run, the Facebook Page AIFeed.run AI News, and LinkedIn.

Stories cover AI models, tools, research, policy, safety and industry news from outlets such as OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Nvidia and major tech publications. Every story points back to its source.

Selection and writing are automated with AI systems. We do not claim a human newsroom rewrites each story. A free weekly AI brief is available by email.
