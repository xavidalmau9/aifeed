Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

AIFeed.run ("we", "us") publishes AI news on AIFeed.run, a free weekly newsletter by email, and social accounts including Instagram @aifeed.run and the Facebook Page "AIFeed.run AI News". This policy explains what data we handle and why.

Website

The AIFeed.run site is static. We do not run our own user accounts or store personal data on the site. Our hosting provider and any embedded services may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type.

Free weekly newsletter

When you subscribe, your email address is collected and stored by our newsletter provider, Beehiiv, and used only to send the brief and related service messages. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Beehiiv's privacy policy: beehiiv.com/privacy.

Instagram and Facebook messages and comments

If you comment on one of our Instagram or Facebook posts or send us a direct message, we receive the data Meta provides through its official APIs, which can include:

  • your public username or Page-scoped / Instagram-scoped user ID,
  • the text of your comment or message and its timestamp,
  • the ID of the post you commented on.

We use this data only to:

  • reply to you, including sending one automated direct message when you comment a keyword (such as "AI") asking for our newsletter link,
  • answer messages you send us,
  • prevent sending you duplicate replies.

Automated replies are sent by software. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than the service providers that run our systems. We keep message and comment records only as long as needed for these purposes and no longer than 90 days, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Meta's own handling of your data is covered by Instagram's and Facebook's privacy policies.

Your choices and data deletion

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time with the link in any email. To ask us to delete data we hold about you, including Instagram or Facebook message and comment records, send a direct message to @aifeed.run on Instagram with the word "DELETE", or contact us via the Facebook Page "AIFeed.run AI News". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
